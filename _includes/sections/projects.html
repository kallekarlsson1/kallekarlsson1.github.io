Eye in the Sky

Vision System for Automatic Detection and Tracking of Flying Objects

Bachelor's thesis · Chalmers University of Technology, in collaboration with Evolved Aerospace · 2026

Can a flying drone reliably detect other drones? This thesis presents a real-time computer vision system that detects, tracks and classifies drones from a moving UAV using both RGB and thermal (infrared) cameras. Because labelled thermal data is scarce, it adapts a CLIP-based classifier to infrared through cross-modal knowledge distillation and LoRA fine-tuning, so it can recognise drones in infrared without any infrared pretraining.

May I Change Your Mind?

The Role of Emotions and Personalization in Overcoming NIMBY Resistance

Bachelor's thesis in Marketing · Gothenburg School of Business, Economics and Law · 2025

Does it matter how a message makes people feel, especially when it makes them feel several things at once? This thesis tests how emotional ambiguity and personalized communication affect NIMBY ("Not In My Backyard") resistance through a controlled online experiment, and proposes a scalable method for measuring emotional ambiguity that combines NLP-based emotion classification with entropy metrics.
